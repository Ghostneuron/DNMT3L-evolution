# Post hoc model-sensitivity addendum

Date: 6 October 2026

## Timing and purpose

This note records two refinements made after the primary sitewise and gene-wide selection summaries were available. They are model-adequacy sensitivities only. They can weaken or qualify a primary result, but they cannot create a candidate or rescue the historical h358 claim.

## Model-matched focal tests

The dated analysis plan called for focused multiple-hit tests at primary robust diversifying candidates. During manuscript audit, the implementation was separated by the model that generated each primary call:

- strict episodic candidates are retested with MEME allowing double and triple nucleotide changes;
- strict pervasive candidates are retested with FEL allowing double and triple nucleotide changes; and
- historical h358 is included in both model families for all four scopes as an audit case.

Each focal test is run separately on the MACSE, MAFFT, and PRANK alignment for its taxonomic scope. P values are Benjamini-Hochberg corrected across all scope-site-alignment tests within each model family. A focal candidate is conditionally retained only when q is at most 0.05 in at least two of the three alignments; the FEL result must also have beta greater than alpha. This separation and cross-aligner rule were specified before the reported focal conditional outputs were generated. HyPhy 2.5.94's site-list helper can also evaluate decimal prefixes of a requested site; exact prespecified rows alone enter the focal summaries and correction, and the evaluated-prefix audit is recorded with each data set. A requested zero-information row is retained as a conservative `p = 1` test rather than omitted. The conditional models do not replace the all-site standard-model discovery analysis and do not retest FUBAR under a multinucleotide model.

## Saturation-gated gene-wide test

After the 12 alignment-level saturation summaries and primary BUSTED results were available, a gene-wide multinucleotide sensitivity was added for MACSE scopes labeled `moderate multiple-hit/saturation risk`. The gate selects Euarchontoglires and Sauropsida and excludes the two scopes labeled `limited multiple-hit signal in sampled pairs`. The selected scopes are rerun with BUSTED allowing double and triple nucleotide changes while retaining synonymous-rate variation.

This gate was not part of the original dated plan and is therefore post hoc. Its value is diagnostic: it asks whether standard-model gene-wide support survives an explicit multinucleotide substitution model in the scopes where the pairwise diagnostic gives the strongest reason to ask. Corrected support or loss of support is reported for both tested scopes.

## Superseded conditional output

An initial conditional MEME execution used the broader union of strict pervasive and episodic candidates. That output used the corrected focal-site syntax and was technically valid, but it mixed candidates generated by different primary model families. It is retained under `analysis_v2/deprecated/conditional_broader_target_set_20261006` for provenance and is excluded from the reported summary. The final MEME output contains strict episodic candidates plus h358; the final FEL output contains strict pervasive candidates plus h358.
